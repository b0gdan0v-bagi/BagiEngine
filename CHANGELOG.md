## 0.1.5 - 2026-11-16 - No autonomous QA: a monkey loop with invariants, shrinking and bug entries

Entry: `qa-loop`. Bump: patch.

### Added

- `tools/qa/bagi_monkey.py`: a seeded monkey runner over the control API; a game's moves come from its `monkey_moves` hook (BagiStress has one).
- The QA loop: a seed log, the signature of a reproduction with a duplicate rule, the command line `next-seeds` / `log-add` / `dupes` / `sweep`, and the skill `/qa-loop`; a found bug becomes an ordinary `bug-*` entry for `todo-loop`.
- `be_add_game_scenario_tests(... INVARIANTS)`: a replay ctest of the stored reproductions (`BagiStressQaReplay`).

## 0.1.4 - 2026-11-16 - Control API: no log counters, no batch and no run-until for automated testers

Entry: `control-qa-conveniences`. Bump: patch.

### Added

- Control API: `status.log` warning and error counters, `log_mark` / `log_since` / `log_subscribe` with a pushed `log` event; `batch` runs a list of calls in one request; `run_until` steps frames until a JSON pointer condition holds.
- `GameSession` and the MCP server expose the same methods; the rule `control-qa-api.md` describes the QA flow.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-15 - Animation: a timeline cannot repeat forever or play back and forth (yoyo): no idle loops, pulses or shimmer

Entry: `timeline-repeat-yoyo`. Bump: patch.

### Added

- `Timeline::Yoyo()`: every second iteration is the exact time reversal of a forward one (clips, delays, callbacks and sub-timelines mirrored).
- `Timeline::RepeatForever()` / `IsEndless()`: an endless timeline; `TweenManager::Play` of one runs until it is cancelled (no `onComplete`, `GetProgress()` stays 0).
- `Timeline::Advance(dt)`: evaluation by the time since the last call, exact for runs of any length.
- `AnimationFrameClass` (`Interactive`, `FiniteEffect`, `DecorativeLoop`), `Timeline::SetFrameClass` / `GetFrameClass`, `TweenHandle::GetFrameClass` and `TweenManager::GetActiveCount(class)`: what keeps the frame loop awake.
