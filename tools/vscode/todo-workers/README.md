# Todo Workers (VS Code)

A panel for the parallel `todo-loop` workers (`tools/todo/worker.py`, "Workers" in `tools/todo/CLAUDE.md`), one card per worker:

- the entry (id, area, title), the state of its background session (busy or idle and how long it runs; done and when its last step was, since a finished session can stay listed by `claude agents` for hours), the ticked boxes of its plan as a bar, the branches of the game and of `engine/`, uncommitted changes;
- **Now** (**Last** once the session is done): the last thing the session said, and the agents it runs (developer, reviewer, ...) with their last step, or their verdict once they are done;
- **Tokens**: what the session and its agents used in total and in the last 10 minutes (output, input, cache write, cache read), the loop against its agents, the context the session holds now, and the tokens by model; above the cards the sum over all workers;
- **Last steps**: the session's last tool calls and messages (folded);
- **waits for you**: when the loop asks a question or a permission (`claude agents`: status `waiting`, passed on by `worker.py list` as `waiting` and `session.waitingFor`) the card gets a red edge, a red badge and a line with the reason and the button **Answer** (`claude attach <session id>` in a terminal; by id, since every session of a worker is named `todo-w<k>`); a notification with **Answer** comes the moment a worker starts waiting, and the status bar counts the waiting workers first, on a red background. A run that only ended its turn (its report closes with an offer; `claude agents` keeps it `blocked` for hours) is no wait: its last answer in the transcript is text with `end_turn` and no tool call (`worker.py list --json`: `endedTurn`), so the card says `done`, not `waits for you`, and **Fast start** stops it and starts the next loop;
- **usage limit**: a loop that the usage limit stopped (its last answer in the transcript is the API's `rate_limit` error, `You've hit your 5-hour limit · resets 10:30pm (...)`; `worker.py list --json`: `limited` {`text`, `resets`, `time`}) gets an orange edge, a badge with the reset time and the button **Relaunch** (`worker.py relaunch <k>`: `claude --bg --resume` of the session with "go on where the loop stopped", so the loop keeps its conversation; press it after the reset); the toolbar has **Relaunch all (N)** while any worker is limited, a notification with **Relaunch** comes when one hits the limit, and the status bar counts them on a yellow background. A session that ended otherwise before its entry reached review (a lost connection) has a plain **Relaunch** among its buttons;
- **stopped**: a session that `worker.py stop` (the **Stop** button) ended has the grey badge `stopped · <when>`, not `done` (`claude agents` lists both as finished, so `stop` keeps a mark of the session id in `~/.claude/todo/stopped-sessions.json`; `worker.py list --json`: `stopped`, ms since the epoch, null for a session that ended by itself or was started again); no "session has ended" notification, the status bar counts them, and **Relaunch** or **Fast start** continues the loop;
- **took nothing**: a loop that ended without claiming any entry (it was blocked before the claim, the entry was taken elsewhere, nothing was takeable) is not `done`: the card gets an amber badge `took nothing` and a line `Took nothing: <id>: <reason>` with **Open entry** and **Fast start**. The entry and the reason come from `worker.py list --store` (`noClaim: {entry, reason}`): the reason is the line `NOT TAKEN <id|->: <reason>` that the loop writes last (`.claude/skills/todo-loop/SKILL.md`); a run that wrote none shows the entry its prompt named and `no reason said: Log shows the run`. The panel keeps no memory of a start of its own. A stopped or usage-limited worker and a worker that holds an entry are never this state;
- **collapse**: cards are folded by default to a few lines (the name, the entry and the badge, the title, the review note or the progress bar, one line of what the loop said, and the one action it needs: Answer, Relaunch, Review or Fast start); the arrow unfolds one, **Expand all** / **Collapse all** every card, and the choice is kept. Waiting and limited workers come first. An unfolded card keeps Attach, Relaunch and Stop or Fast start in sight; Log, Plan, Build & run, Update, Folder and Terminal are behind `…`;
- buttons: **Attach** (`claude attach <session id>`; **Answer** takes its place while the session waits), **Log** (`claude logs`), **Folder** (a new window), **Terminal**, **Plan** (`~/.claude/plans/todo-loop-<branch-id>.md`), **Stop** (`worker.py stop <k>`, after a question, while the session runs); while it does not: **Update** (`worker.py update <k>`: the game and `engine/` detached at the tips of their base branches, as the loop's "go to the base"; the branch it held is kept; refused with uncommitted changes) and **Fast start** (`worker.py start <k>` at once: `/todo-loop` with the settings `todoWorkers.startSlices`, `todoWorkers.loopArgs` and `todoWorkers.reportTo`).

Added in 0.26 (the stopped run, the progress of a through entry, the guards of the panel):

- **Took nothing** (see the bullet above): the flow graph draws the worker box `took nothing` and, in place of the ghost `no entry`, an amber dashed box of the tried entry (`not taken · <reason>`; `-` shows `nothing eligible`; the tooltip has the full reason and whether the loop said it or the entry is only the one the run was started on); a click opens that entry in the Todo list. The feed gets one line `<worker>: the loop took nothing: ...` and a warning notification with **Show log** the moment the state appears.
- **Progress of a through entry** comes from the store's `slices` of the entry (`by: "stages"` when it has a stage plan, `stagesPlan` names it): `stage 2/3 · this slice 12/30`, the bar fills with the stages approved; `this slice` is left out when the plan shown is the stage plan itself. Without a stage plan only `this slice d/t` (the sum of old slice plans, which showed `72/74`, is not shown). The panel reads no stage plan of its own. Cards, the graph and the Todo list use one function (`progressText` in `media/runs.js`).
- **Queue guards**: **Queue for worker** calls `worker.py queue <k> --add <id>` and shows the CLI's refusal with its reason. `blocked by ...` (a stacked or paired entry is takeable, so only the others) asks `Queue anyway?`, and a yes repeats the call with `--force`; `taken by <origin>` (the branch lives in another checkout) and `closed` are refused with no offer, because a start would drop them again. Dragging to reorder adds every id of the new order with `--force` (an add moves a queued id to the end), so nothing is cleared and nothing can be lost; a reorder does not resume a paused queue.
- **Quick merge** passes `--claude` to `worker.py merge`, so the CLI sees the loops: its `warnings` (a worker runs a loop on the base the merge moves: `w<k> runs a loop on <base> ...`) are listed in the detail of the dialog, which is still the owner's yes, and its `problems` (a worker's loop holds the branch: `runs a loop on <branch>`, the merge would detach it) are refused with their text, as conflicts are. The panel keeps no check of its own; the warnings of the merge itself join the notes of the message after it.
- **`waits for <id>, no link`**: an entry whose body says `**Waits for:** <id>` while `blockedBy` does not hold it gets an amber chip with **Link** (`todo.py dep add`) and a `!` on the folded row. The panel does not read the bodies itself: the rows `waits-unlinked` of `worker.py doctor --json` (its `store` list; the CLI skips a blocker the entry is stacked or paired on) come with the slower views (every 5 min, a minute after a write), so a new entry's chip can lag by up to a minute. The Todo list's own dependency edits (add, link, remove) read it at once; a read that failed or has not come shows no chip.
- **Tests**: `node --test 'tools/vscode/todo-workers/test/*.test.js'` from the engine root (the quoted glob; Node 26 does not take the folder). They drive the pure functions of `media/runs.js` and a rendered graph; no VS Code, no npm.

Added in 0.25 (the machine; `worker.py machine`: one `ps` and one `sysctl`, about 0.1 s of CPU, read beside every refresh, hidden too):

- **Machine line** under the toolbar: the 1-minute load against the cores, memory in use, the memory pressure when it is not normal, the swap; amber when the load is above 1.5 x the cores or the pressure is not normal.
- **On a worker card**: what its folder runs now (compiling N, linking N, tests N, build), its CPU and memory. A process goes to the folder its command line names (`<name>.workers/w<k>/...` or a main checkout `<name>/...`); a build or test tool that names none (ninja, ctest) takes the folder of its jobs, then of its parent.
- **Machine** (folded section): every folder at work on this machine, the other games' checkouts and workers too (their builds and tests take the same cores), and the Claude processes with their memory. ps's CPU is an average and misses short compiler runs; the load counts them.
- **The load on the Timeline**: the panel samples the load and the memory once a minute into its state (about three days kept) and draws them under the lanes, with the cores as a dashed line, so a slow stretch can be matched with what ran then.

Added in 0.24 (the load):

- **Day, Timeline, Busy files and the review rounds are cached**: each view keeps its last answer (also across a reload, in the extension's state) and is read again on its own interval while the panel is visible (Day 2 min, Timeline and Busy files 5 min, review rounds 15 min), after a write to the store at the soonest 30 s to 2 min later (one read for a burst of writes; the timeline does not follow the store), and at once on **Read again**. A section says how old its answer is and `reading …` while a newer one comes; only a view never read waits empty. `worker.py timeline` keeps the spans of finished sessions in `~/.claude/todo/timeline-cache.json` by the transcript's size, so it parses only the running and new ones.
- **The refresh**: the panel keeps what it shows while `worker.py list` runs (no more empty sections every 15 s). Hidden, it reads every `todoWorkers.hiddenRefreshSeconds` (60) and without `--activity --usage` (no transcripts: the status bar and the notifications do not need them); visible, every `todoWorkers.refreshSeconds` (15) in full. A write to the store still reads at once.

Added in 0.23 (the data: `worker.py doctor` and `list --json --store`: `problems`, `diff`; the slower views of `worker.py`):

- **Doctor**: a card with a problem gets an amber edge and a line per problem, folded or not, with **Fix** when `worker.py doctor <k> --fix <kind>` can repair it: two loops running in one folder (stops all but the oldest, whose claim won), commits on a detached HEAD on no branch (moves the entry's branch forward to them, a fast-forward only, or keeps them on `rescue/w<k>-<sha>`), a worker left on a branch whose entry is not taken (detaches it), uncommitted changes with no loop running, a loop that has not claimed its named entry after 15 minutes.
- **Diff**: an entry in progress shows what its branch holds so far (`N commits: files, +, -` and what is not committed); **Diff** behind `…` opens the commits in one diff editor.
- **Merge all (N)** in the toolbar while two or more review entries wait for nothing: `worker.py merge-train` merges them one by one (engine entries first, then the fewest conflicts with the other review branches; an entry that waits for another of the train goes right after it), each planned again on the moved base, stops at the first conflict or refusal (the merges before it stay), kicks the workers once, and Verify builds and tests the result once. The dialog shows the order first.
- **Day** (folded section): what was merged since the start of the day (midnight, or the last **End the day**), what is in review and in progress, the queues, the review rounds of the last 7 days, and the base branches (`main`, `games/*`) of the game and of `engine/` ahead of or behind their upstream as the last fetch left it (no fetch), with the `git push` commands, engine first (**Copy push commands**; the panel never pushes). **End the day** empties every worker queue (`worker.py queue --clear`; running loops finish their slice) and copies the commands. **Dispatch** runs `claude '/todo-dispatch'` in the main checkout.
- **Timeline** (folded section, `todoWorkers.timelineHours`, 24): one lane per worker, a bar per session from its transcript (its entry: the `/todo-loop` prompt, else its claim), coloured by how it ended (reached review, a through slice approved, running, usage limit, ended without review), the busy share per worker and a warning when two sessions of one lane overlap.
- **Busy files** (folded section, `worker.py busy`): each taken entry with its busy files by folder (todo-loop, "Busy files"; paths relative to the area's repository), and the open entries grouped by what keeps them from a worker: the taken entries their files meet, free, or files unknown (no cached verdict, no paths in the text). A click on an id opens it in the Todo list.
- **Review rounds**: a review card says how many reviews the entry had, how often the reviewer sent a slice back (`-r2` plans) and the owner's remarks rounds (`worker.py review-stats`).
- **Push to a phone**: `todoWorkers.pushUrl` (empty: off) gets a POST with the text of what stops a worker (it waits for you, it hit the usage limit, an entry reached review), the title in the `Title` header: an ntfy topic URL puts it on a phone. The text names entry ids and worker names.

A dashed arrow runs from a blocker shown in the graph to the entry it blocks: `stacked on` (cut from the blocker's branch in review), `paired` (the engine's branch in review, the game's entry), `waits for` / `waits for merge` (closed first). Titles are truncated (the full text, the wait and the blockers are in the tooltip); the graph scrolls sideways in a narrow panel.
- **On the graph**: a click on a worker or the entry it holds unfolds its card (in the tab: unfolds the entry in the list); a click on a queued entry opens it in the Todo list; `×` takes it out of the queue; dragging a queued box along its lane reorders the queue (both views). The flat `next:` row of the cards is gone.

One `todo.py list` serves every queue.

Added in 0.17:

Every write is `todo.py --actor owner-panel`. The queues of the workers stand above the list; dragging an entry reorders its worker's queue.

Added in 0.16 (the data from `worker.py`, the actions in the panel):

- **What a merge meets** (`list --store`: `conflicts`, `behind`, `riskFiles`, `conflictsWith`): a review card names the files that conflict with the base, the other review branches it conflicts with (merging one first makes the other need a hand), how far the base moved since the cut and which of the branch's files those commits changed (no git conflict, but where a merge breaks), and the entries that wait on it.
- **Merge + test**: Quick merge, then `cmake --preset`, the build of the product and the tests and `ctest` in the main checkout as a VS Code task; the result (green or red, at which commit) stays on the panel and in the feed. **Verify** runs the same at any time. The preset and targets: `worker.py build-info` (the platform's debug preset, `Product` and `Tests` of `.claude/rules/build-project.md`; `todoWorkers.preset` overrides).
- **Unblocked**: after a merge that closes an entry, the entries whose last open blocker it was (`merge --json`: `unblocked`) come in a notification with **Start** (a free worker) and **Queue**.
- **Queues** (`worker.py queue`, `~/.claude/todo/worker-queues.json`): **Queue for worker** in the Todo list puts an open entry in a worker's queue; the card shows it (× takes it out), and the panel starts the next one as soon as the worker is free (no session, clean, its entry in review or none; `todoWorkers.autoStartQueue`) with `worker.py start <k> --queued-only`: the CLI takes the first entry it can start, drops a closed one or one taken elsewhere with its reason, puts the entry back when the start fails and pauses the queue after 4 entries in a row without the owner (the card shows the pause). What it printed about the queue (`dropped`, `stays queued`, paused, nothing takeable) goes to the feed as `queue` lines. A queue whose waits did not change since the last call that started nothing is not asked again, so a blocked queue costs one call. Queueing and **Attach to free worker** first show the busy entries whose slice plans name files the entry names (`worker.py overlap`, a hint from the entry's text).
- **What it asks**: a waiting card shows the pending tool call (the command of a permission, the questions of `AskUserQuestion`; `list`: `asking`).
- **Feed** ("What happened", kept across reloads): reached review, waits, limit, ended, merged, unblocked, verify green or red, queued and started, what a queue start dropped or left, pointer commits. The loops' reports land here even when `reportTo` names no session.
- **Build & run** (worker and review cards): configure, build and start the product in the worker folder that holds the branch. The window has the engine's size (no command line option sets another yet).
- **Shots** (review cards, when the repository has `tools/ui_shots.py`): the tool on the branch (its worker) and on the base (the main checkout) into `~/.claude/plans/review-shots/<id>/{after,before}`, then a tab with the pictures side by side.
- **Dollars**: tokens priced with `todoWorkers.prices` (API list prices by model family; a subscription bills otherwise) on every usage block, and per entry over every loop session that held it (`~/.claude/todo/usage-ledger.json`; `list --usage`: `entryUsage`).
- **The engine pointer** (in a game; `list`: `pointer`): the recorded commit against `engine/`'s checkout and its base, and **Commit pointer** (one commit of `engine/` only, `Update engine: <subject>`, after a confirmation) when they differ.

Below them the entries in `review` of the areas of `.claude/rules/todo-project.md` (other games' entries in the store are left out), with:

- **Review**: the changes of the branch against the point where it left its base, every file in one multi-diff editor (a terminal with `git diff --stat` when the editor cannot show it);
- **Review with Claude**: a terminal in the main checkout runs `claude` with a review prompt: it reads the entry (and its plan) and `git diff <base>...<branch>`, checks them against the entry and the repository's `CLAUDE.md` and rules, and reports findings with `file:line` and a verdict. It is told to stay read only (no checkout, edit, commit, merge or store change); a second click shows the same terminal;
- **Quick merge**: `worker.py merge <id>` without Claude. A dialog shows the commits and what follows (the entry closed, or open again for the next slice; the worker that holds the branch detached; the branch deleted; for the engine in a game the commit `Update engine: <title>`); a yes does it. Conflicts, uncommitted changes and an entry not in review are refused with nothing changed. It never builds: when the base moved since the branch was cut, the dialog says so and the tests are the owner's;
- the card says "taken in <origin>, base <base>" (the `base` and `origin` that `todo.py claim --base` stored; `worker.py list --store`: `takenOrigin`, `takenBase`). An entry whose branch is not in this checkout (taken in another checkout or on another machine), or was cut from another base (`games/chess` while the area's base is `games/mahjong`), is not this checkout's: it leaves the list, the count in the status bar and the "reached review" notice, and the folded line **N in other checkouts** under the cards names it with its origin and base (`local`, `foreignBase`; `worker.py merge` refuses it too). It is recognised by the stored base; an entry claimed before the store kept it is recognised by the commits the branch holds (a guess);
- **Merge with Claude**: a terminal in the main checkout runs `claude '/todo-merge <branch>'`: the skill reports, asks, and builds and tests when the base moved (a second click shows the same terminal);
- **Plan**.

Above the cards, next to **Refresh** and **Settings**, **Todo list** opens a tab of the editor with the entries of the todo store (`todo.py list --all --json --body`): the areas of this checkout by default (or one area, or all), chips per status with counts (closed ones off at first), a search over id, title and text; within a status by priority (0.18 above), then newest first. A click on an entry unfolds its text (paragraphs, bullets, bold and code; everything as text, no HTML) with **Markdown** (`todo.py show` in a markdown preview), **Plan** (the local copy in `~/.claude/plans`, else `todo.py plan get`) and **Copy id**. An open entry, and one in progress that no running worker holds, has **Attach to free worker**: a dialog names the free workers (no session running, no uncommitted changes; those that hold no entry first; for an entry whose branch an idle worker holds, only that worker) and a mode dialog comes first (0.18 above), and the worker picked runs `worker.py start <k> --delay 0 --slices <N of the mode> --loop-args '<id> <todoWorkers.loopArgs>'` (and `--report-to`), so its loop takes exactly that entry (or continues its branch); the button is greyed out while no worker is free, and an entry a running worker holds shows that worker; one whose branch lives elsewhere (above) is hidden until the chip **other checkouts N** is on, and then shows `branch elsewhere` (its tooltip names the origin and the base when stored) and has no button. An unfolded entry in progress or in review shows "taken in <origin>, base <base>" when the store has them. It reads the store when it opens, on **Refresh** and on every write signal; its writes are those of 0.17 and 0.18 above.

The notification of a new entry in review offers **Review**, **Review with Claude**, **Quick merge** and **Merge with Claude**. A line in the status bar counts the workers that wait for you, the busy and idle ones and the entries in review, and a notification comes when a worker starts waiting for you (**Answer**), an entry reaches review or a session ends.

**Updates.** `todo.py` rewrites `~/.claude/todo/changed.json` after every write the store accepts (a loop's claim, review or plan, a merge, a close, the owner's CLI), and the panel watches that folder: a change reads the workers and the Todo list tab again at once (writes within 0.7 s are one refresh). The timer below stays for what moves without a write (sessions, their steps and tokens); the Todo list tab also reads the store again when it is shown again, for edits made in Vikunja.

It reads `worker.py list --json --store --activity --usage` every 15 s from the first workspace folder that has `tools/todo/worker.py` or `engine/tools/todo/worker.py` (the main checkout); the activity and the tokens come from the session transcripts in `~/.claude/projects/`. Everything shown is put in as text. It writes what the owner asks for (a merge, remarks, the edits of the Todo list) and starts loops only on a button or from a queue; `/todo-loop-parallel` starts them all.

## Install

No npm: the packer uses the standard library.

```bash
python3 tools/vscode/todo-workers/pack.py        # in a game: engine/tools/vscode/todo-workers/pack.py
code --install-extension tools/vscode/todo-workers/build/todo-workers-0.26.0.vsix
```

`code` is in `/Applications/Visual Studio Code.app/Contents/Resources/app/bin/` on macOS when it is not on PATH. Then **Developer: Reload Window**. After a change bump `version` in `package.json`, pack and install again.

## Settings

`todoWorkers.python` (`python3`, at least 3.10; a bare name is looked up with `~/.local/bin`, Homebrew and `/usr/local/bin` ahead of the system folders, whose `python3` on macOS is 3.9), `todoWorkers.claude` (`claude`; a full path when VS Code started from the Dock does not see it: the panel says "claude agents did not answer"), `todoWorkers.refreshSeconds` (15), `todoWorkers.notify` (true).
