# Todo Workers (VS Code)

A panel for the parallel `todo-loop` workers (`tools/todo/worker.py`, "Workers" in `tools/todo/CLAUDE.md`), one card per worker:

- the entry (id, area, title), the state of its background session (busy or idle and how long it runs; done and when its last step was, since a finished session can stay listed by `claude agents` for hours), the ticked boxes of its plan as a bar, the branches of the game and of `engine/`, uncommitted changes;
- **Now** (**Last** once the session is done): the last thing the session said, and the agents it runs (developer, reviewer, ...) with their last step, or their verdict once they are done;
- **Tokens**: what the session and its agents used in total and in the last 10 minutes (output, input, cache write, cache read), the loop against its agents, the context the session holds now, and the tokens by model; above the cards the sum over all workers;
- **Last steps**: the session's last tool calls and messages (folded);
- **waits for you**: when the loop asks a question or a permission (`claude agents`: status `waiting`, passed on by `worker.py list` as `waiting` and `session.waitingFor`) the card gets a red edge, a red badge and a line with the reason and the button **Answer** (`claude attach <session id>` in a terminal; by id, since every session of a worker is named `todo-w<k>`); a notification with **Answer** comes the moment a worker starts waiting, and the status bar counts the waiting workers first, on a red background. A run that only ended its turn (its report closes with an offer; `claude agents` keeps it `blocked` for hours) is no wait: its last answer in the transcript is text with `end_turn` and no tool call (`worker.py list --json`: `endedTurn`), so the card says `done`, not `waits for you`, and **Fast start** stops it and starts the next loop;
- **usage limit**: a loop that the usage limit stopped (its last answer in the transcript is the API's `rate_limit` error, `You've hit your 5-hour limit · resets 10:30pm (...)`; `worker.py list --json`: `limited` {`text`, `resets`, `time`}) gets an orange edge, a badge with the reset time and the button **Relaunch** (`worker.py relaunch <k>`: `claude --bg --resume` of the session with "go on where the loop stopped", so the loop keeps its conversation; press it after the reset); the toolbar has **Relaunch all (N)** while any worker is limited, a notification with **Relaunch** comes when one hits the limit, and the status bar counts them on a yellow background. A session that ended otherwise before its entry reached review (a lost connection) has a plain **Relaunch** among its buttons;
- **stopped**: a session that `worker.py stop` (the **Stop** button) ended has the grey badge `stopped · <when>`, not `done` (`claude agents` lists both as finished, so `stop` keeps a mark of the session id in `~/.claude/todo/stopped-sessions.json`; `worker.py list --json`: `stopped`, ms since the epoch, null for a session that ended by itself or was started again); no "session has ended" notification, the status bar counts them, and **Relaunch** or **Fast start** continues the loop;
- **collapse**: cards are folded by default to a few lines (the name, the entry and the badge, the title, the review note or the progress bar, one line of what the loop said, and the one action it needs: Answer, Relaunch, Review or Fast start); the arrow unfolds one, **Expand all** / **Collapse all** every card, and the choice is kept. Waiting and limited workers come first. An unfolded card keeps Attach, Relaunch and Stop or Fast start in sight; Log, Plan, Build & run, Update, Folder and Terminal are behind `…`;
- buttons: **Attach** (`claude attach <session id>`; **Answer** takes its place while the session waits), **Log** (`claude logs`), **Folder** (a new window), **Terminal**, **Plan** (`~/.claude/plans/todo-loop-<branch-id>.md`), **Stop** (`worker.py stop <k>`, after a question, while the session runs); while it does not: **Update** (`worker.py update <k>`: the game and `engine/` detached at the tips of their base branches, as the loop's "go to the base"; the branch it held is kept; refused with uncommitted changes) and **Fast start** (`worker.py start <k>` at once: `/todo-loop` with the settings `todoWorkers.startSlices`, `todoWorkers.loopArgs` and `todoWorkers.reportTo`).

Added in 0.17:

- **Through and money**: a card's bar covers the whole entry when it has several slice plans (`3 slices 120/190 · this slice 12/30`); the toggle `slice by slice` / `through` sets the entry's `slicing` (`todo.py edit --slicing`; turning it on asks for an optional cost cap), and the dollars of the entry stand beside it against the cap.
- **Return with remarks** (review cards, and the notification of a new review): a draft opens in the editor (`~/.claude/plans/remarks-draft-<id>.md`); **Send the remarks** runs `worker.py remarks <id> --start`, which stores them, sets the entry back to `in progress` and starts (or queues first) the worker that holds the branch: the loop fixes them on the same branch and brings it back to review (`todo-loop/SKILL.md`, "Remarks"). A review card names the blockers to merge first.
- **Writes from the Todo list**: **New entry** (area, title with a hint of entries with a similar title, id, the body in a draft), and on an unfolded entry **Defer** / **Open**, **Stage**, **Edit text** (refused when the entry changed meanwhile), **Through**, its dependencies (`× ` removes one, **+ waits for** adds one). Every write is `todo.py --actor owner-panel`. The queues of the workers stand above the list; dragging an entry reorders its worker's queue.
- **Modules**: `extension.js` wires `src/tools.js` (settings, the tools, PATH), `src/feed.js`, `src/build.js`, `src/workers.js`, `src/review.js` and `src/todoList.js` around one shared context; each module adds its actions to it.

Added in 0.16 (the data from `worker.py`, the actions in the panel):

- **What a merge meets** (`list --store`: `conflicts`, `behind`, `riskFiles`, `conflictsWith`): a review card names the files that conflict with the base, the other review branches it conflicts with (merging one first makes the other need a hand), how far the base moved since the cut and which of the branch's files those commits changed (no git conflict, but where a merge breaks), and the entries that wait on it.
- **Merge + test**: Quick merge, then `cmake --preset`, the build of the product and the tests and `ctest` in the main checkout as a VS Code task; the result (green or red, at which commit) stays on the panel and in the feed. **Verify** runs the same at any time. The preset and targets: `worker.py build-info` (the platform's debug preset, `Product` and `Tests` of `.claude/rules/build-project.md`; `todoWorkers.preset` overrides).
- **Unblocked**: after a merge that closes an entry, the entries whose last open blocker it was (`merge --json`: `unblocked`) come in a notification with **Start** (a free worker) and **Queue**.
- **Queues** (`worker.py queue`, `~/.claude/todo/worker-queues.json`): **Queue for worker** in the Todo list puts an open entry in a worker's queue; the card shows it (× takes it out), and the panel starts the next one as soon as the worker is free (no session, clean, its entry in review or none; `todoWorkers.autoStartQueue`). Queueing and **Attach to free worker** first show the busy entries whose slice plans name files the entry names (`worker.py overlap`, a hint from the entry's text).
- **What it asks**: a waiting card shows the pending tool call (the command of a permission, the questions of `AskUserQuestion`; `list`: `asking`).
- **Feed** ("What happened", kept across reloads): reached review, waits, limit, ended, merged, unblocked, verify green or red, queued and started, pointer commits. The loops' reports land here even when `reportTo` names no session.
- **Build & run** (worker and review cards): configure, build and start the product in the worker folder that holds the branch. The window has the engine's size (no command line option sets another yet).
- **Shots** (review cards, when the repository has `tools/ui_shots.py`): the tool on the branch (its worker) and on the base (the main checkout) into `~/.claude/plans/review-shots/<id>/{after,before}`, then a tab with the pictures side by side.
- **Dollars**: tokens priced with `todoWorkers.prices` (API list prices by model family; a subscription bills otherwise) on every usage block, and per entry over every loop session that held it (`~/.claude/todo/usage-ledger.json`; `list --usage`: `entryUsage`).
- **The engine pointer** (in a game; `list`: `pointer`): the recorded commit against `engine/`'s checkout and its base, and **Commit pointer** (one commit of `engine/` only, `Update engine: <subject>`, after a confirmation) when they differ.

Below them the entries in `review` of the areas of `.claude/rules/todo-project.md` (other games' entries in the store are left out), with:

- **Review**: the changes of the branch against the point where it left its base, every file in one multi-diff editor (a terminal with `git diff --stat` when the editor cannot show it);
- **Review with Claude**: a terminal in the main checkout runs `claude` with a review prompt: it reads the entry (and its plan) and `git diff <base>...<branch>`, checks them against the entry and the repository's `CLAUDE.md` and rules, and reports findings with `file:line` and a verdict. It is told to stay read only (no checkout, edit, commit, merge or store change); a second click shows the same terminal;
- **Quick merge**: `worker.py merge <id>` without Claude. A dialog shows the commits and what follows (the entry closed, or open again for the next slice; the worker that holds the branch detached; the branch deleted; for the engine in a game the commit `Update engine: <title>`); a yes does it. Conflicts, uncommitted changes and an entry not in review are refused with nothing changed. It never builds: when the base moved since the branch was cut, the dialog says so and the tests are the owner's;
- the card says "taken in <origin>, base <base>" (the `base` and `origin` that `todo.py claim --base` stored; `worker.py list --store`: `takenOrigin`, `takenBase`). An entry whose branch is not in this checkout (taken in another checkout or on another machine), or was cut from another base (`games/chess` while the area's base is `games/mahjong`), says so on its card, and these four buttons are greyed out (`local`, `foreignBase`; `worker.py merge` refuses it too). It is recognised by the stored base; an entry claimed before the store kept it is recognised by the commits the branch holds (a guess);
- **Merge with Claude**: a terminal in the main checkout runs `claude '/todo-merge <branch>'`: the skill reports, asks, and builds and tests when the base moved (a second click shows the same terminal);
- **Plan**.

Above the cards, next to **Refresh** and **Settings**, **Todo list** opens a tab of the editor with the entries of the todo store (`todo.py list --all --json --body`): the areas of this checkout by default (or one area, or all), chips per status with counts (closed ones off at first), a search over id, title and text; newest first within a status. A click on an entry unfolds its text (paragraphs, bullets, bold and code; everything as text, no HTML) with **Markdown** (`todo.py show` in a markdown preview), **Plan** (the local copy in `~/.claude/plans`, else `todo.py plan get`) and **Copy id**. An open entry, and one in progress that no running worker holds, has **Attach to free worker**: a dialog names the free workers (no session running, no uncommitted changes; those that hold no entry first; for an entry whose branch an idle worker holds, only that worker) and the one picked runs `worker.py start <k> --delay 0 --slices 1 --loop-args '<id> <todoWorkers.loopArgs>'` (and `--report-to`), so its loop takes exactly that entry (or continues its branch); the button is greyed out while no worker is free, and an entry a running worker holds shows that worker; one whose branch lives elsewhere (above) shows `branch elsewhere` (its tooltip names the origin and the base when stored) and has no button. An unfolded entry in progress or in review shows "taken in <origin>, base <base>" when the store has them. It reads the store when it opens, on **Refresh** and on every write signal; its writes are those of 0.17 above.

The notification of a new entry in review offers **Review**, **Review with Claude**, **Quick merge** and **Merge with Claude**. A line in the status bar counts the workers that wait for you, the busy and idle ones and the entries in review, and a notification comes when a worker starts waiting for you (**Answer**), an entry reaches review or a session ends.

**Updates.** `todo.py` rewrites `~/.claude/todo/changed.json` after every write the store accepts (a loop's claim, review or plan, a merge, a close, the owner's CLI), and the panel watches that folder: a change reads the workers and the Todo list tab again at once (writes within 0.7 s are one refresh). The timer below stays for what moves without a write (sessions, their steps and tokens); the Todo list tab also reads the store again when it is shown again, for edits made in Vikunja.

It reads `worker.py list --json --store --activity --usage` every 15 s from the first workspace folder that has `tools/todo/worker.py` or `engine/tools/todo/worker.py` (the main checkout); the activity and the tokens come from the session transcripts in `~/.claude/projects/`. Everything shown is put in as text. It writes what the owner asks for (a merge, remarks, the edits of the Todo list) and starts loops only on a button or from a queue; `/todo-loop-parallel` starts them all.

## Install

No npm: the packer uses the standard library.

```bash
python3 tools/vscode/todo-workers/pack.py        # in a game: engine/tools/vscode/todo-workers/pack.py
code --install-extension tools/vscode/todo-workers/build/todo-workers-0.17.0.vsix
```

`code` is in `/Applications/Visual Studio Code.app/Contents/Resources/app/bin/` on macOS when it is not on PATH. Then **Developer: Reload Window**. After a change bump `version` in `package.json`, pack and install again.

## Settings

`todoWorkers.python` (`python3`, at least 3.10; a bare name is looked up with `~/.local/bin`, Homebrew and `/usr/local/bin` ahead of the system folders, whose `python3` on macOS is 3.9), `todoWorkers.claude` (`claude`; a full path when VS Code started from the Dock does not see it: the panel says "claude agents did not answer"), `todoWorkers.refreshSeconds` (15), `todoWorkers.notify` (true).
