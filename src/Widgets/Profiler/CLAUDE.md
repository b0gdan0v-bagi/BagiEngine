# Profiler panel

The editor's view of the frame statistics, the zone ring, the spikes, the heap and the Chrome trace: the asset `config/panels/ProfilerPanel.xml` (window `Profiler###Profiler`, hidden at start, `AssetPanelWidget` hosts it; format and interpreter: `src/Widgets/PanelAssets/CLAUDE.md`) over `ProfilerPanelModel` and six slots. What the numbers mean and how the ring, the spikes and the backends work: `BECore/Profiler/CLAUDE.md`. Editor only: the folder is in the `BE_WITH_EDITOR` regex of `src/CMakeLists.txt` (`.claude/rules/widgets-module.md`).

- **Model and view.** `ProfilerPanelModel` (no ImGui, `IPanelModel`; the asset's source `profiler`) holds the state of the old widget: the frame history and one ring snapshot per frame, the spike capture, the selected frame, the opened spike and the trace commands. Its reflected `ProfilerPanelView` has two blocks. The controls write the first with **no command**, and the model reads it in its next `Update`: so a value the model needs at once is read there, and the model clamps what a control wrote to `ProfilerPanelLimits` (the XML says the same ranges) and the combo indices to their label lists. The model writes the second block (the label lists, `hasFrames`, `zonesRecorded`, `allocTracked`, `recording`, the spike flags and texts, `traceMessage`); its texts are made again only when what they show moved. The combos are lists of texts chosen by an index, not enums.
- **Spike settings** go to `SpikeCapture::SetSettings` only on a real change. Spikes are judged on every fresh snapshot, paused or not; Pause keeps the picture the panel shows (`_snapshot`), not the capture.
- **The ring runs only while the panel is open.** `OnOpenChanged` of `IPanelModel` switches it on and off, whoever changed the open flag (the command, the close button, the asset's `open`); the model is not updated while closed, so it could not see the close button. The destructor switches off what the model switched on, since a mode switch can destroy the widget in the middle of the run. Zones exist only in a build with `ENGINE_PROFILER`: a section that needs them is hidden by `zonesRecorded` and its note says which preset to use.
- **The allocation pause** is held from `Update` to `EndUpdate`, so the panel's own work (history, lanes, ImGui's memory) is not in the counts it shows. The destructor releases a pause still held; a test that checks a frame allocates nothing turns the pause off (`SetPausesAllocCounting`), which would hide what it checks.
- **Ctrl+Shift+P** is read from the raw SDL event by the model, which is alive while the panel is closed, and sent as `view.profiler`, which the asset's `command` turns into the open flag. A table hotkey could not do it: the chord must work while an ImGui text field has the keyboard. The overlay in Game mode reads the same chord on its own.
- **Commands** (rows of the table, run only while the panel is open): `profiler.trace` with a literal `argument` of the button (`record`, `restart`, `write`, `writeFrames`; an unknown or empty one does nothing), `profiler.spikeLive`, `profiler.spikeClear`, `profiler.resetZoneAllocs`. `writeFrames` writes the frozen spikes, or else the selected or newest frame, to `tracePath`. Record switches the **process-wide** Chrome backend; the model never switches it back on its own.
- **Slots** (`ProfilerSlots`, a member of the model, registered in its `Initialize`; the handles end with the model) keep what the format cannot say: `profilerFrames` (the bars with their tooltip and click, the legend, the work / wait summary, the counters and the overlay lines), `profilerTimeline` (the whole panel, its Fit / Follow newest toolbar included: it shares a line with a readout computed from the view and the frame), `profilerZones` (the table with its sort and the "Timeline" answer), `profilerSpikes` (the list of frozen frames), `profilerMemory`, `profilerZoneAllocs`. A slot draws from the model's state and reports a click back through `SelectFrame`, `OpenSpike`, `ShowLive` and `ShowWorstCall`; the timeline panel lives in the slot, so the model hands it a `ProfilerTimelineRequest` (reset, mark a call) that the slot takes once per draw. A slot checks nothing about the build: the asset hides a section that has nothing to show.
- **Zero allocations in a steady frame:** the snapshot is copied into a second one and the two are swapped, so both keep their capacity; the history vector, the texts and the lanes are members. `ASteadyUpdateAllocatesNothing` bites only with `ENGINE_PROFILER_ALLOC` (the profile preset).
- **Tests:** `ProfilerPanelModelTest` (the model over a ring and a collector of its own: clamps, spike settings, pause, the commands, the chord, the ring and the pause pairs, no allocation), `ProfilerPanelAssetTest` (the shipped file drawn over the model and the slots in an ImGui context with no backend: Pause, Work only, the custom budget number and its clamp, the combos, the filter, the spike mode, Live, a Write click and a button's width). The zone sections are shown in a build with no zones by setting the view's flag, since the slots read the model and its ring. Not covered: the look, the click on a bar, the timeline's mouse handling and the real Chrome backend (Record is never clicked in a test); look at them by hand in an editor build (`macos-profile-editor` for the zone parts).
