## Changed
- The `cpp-senior-programmer` skill is a thin map: per task, the nested `CLAUDE.md` or rule to read and a real file to copy, the traps a change outside a folder walks into, a self-check of a diff and the end-of-plan steps. Its `patterns.md` holds idioms that exist in the code, each with its file. It no longer teaches names the engine does not have (`BE_ASSERT`, `NewAtomic`, `StrongTypedef`, direct `eastl::`, `MyEvent::Subscribe`).
